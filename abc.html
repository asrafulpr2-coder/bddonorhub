About JADCian Donor Hub

Connecting blood donors with those in need since 2020

Our Mission

JADCian Donor Hub is dedicated to creating a reliable network of blood donors to ensure timely access to safe blood for patients in need across Bangladesh.

What We Do

  • Connect blood donors with recipients
  • Maintain an up-to-date donor database
  • Provide eligibility information
  • Promote regular blood donation
  • Organize blood donation camps
  • Educate about blood donation benefits

Our Vision

To create a Bangladesh where no one dies due to unavailability of blood, and where blood donation becomes a regular part of our culture.
